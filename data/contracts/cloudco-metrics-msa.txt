MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between
CloudCo Metrics ("Provider") and Meridian Retail LLP ("Customer").

1. SERVICES. Provider will supply the CloudCo Metrics analytics platform,
Professional tier, at a subscription fee of $299.00 per month, invoiced monthly.

2. TERM. The Initial Term of this Agreement begins on January 1, 2026 and ends
on December 31, 2026.

3. RENEWAL. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12)
month terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least
sixty (60) days prior to the end of the then-current term. Notice of
non-renewal must be delivered in writing to cancellations@cloudcometrics.test.

4. FEES ON RENEWAL. Provider may increase subscription fees by up to seven
percent (7%) upon each renewal.

5. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
Delaware.
