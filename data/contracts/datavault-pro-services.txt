SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made between DataVault Pro
("Company") and Meridian Retail LLP ("Client").

1. SERVICES. Company provides managed backup and archival storage, Business
plan, at a fee of $540.00 per month, invoiced monthly in advance.

2. INITIAL TERM. The Initial Term commences on March 1, 2026 and expires on
February 28, 2027.

3. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL. Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement
will renew automatically for successive one-year periods unless Client
delivers written notice of termination no later than ninety (90) days prior
to the expiration of the then-current term. All termination notices must be
sent to accounts@datavaultpro.test.

4. EARLY TERMINATION. Termination outside the notice window obligates Client
to pay all fees for the remainder of the then-current term.

5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between
the parties.
